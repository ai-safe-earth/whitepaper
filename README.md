## What this is

Three rings of defence around artificial intelligence already exist — around the model,
around the product, around states. This paper argues for the fourth: around communities.

It sets out an integral conception of AI safety, one that includes the technical,
regulatory and educational dimensions but centres something usually left out — the
physicality of human relationships. The offline territory, the social infrastructure and
the shared physical space, treated as critical infrastructure for resilience against
the risks a powerful AI layer introduces: disinformation, labour disruption, the erosion
of human agency, and the concentration of power.

An AI safe territory is not a refuge, not a bunker, and not a village without wifi. It
is a social structure that lets a place live alongside a technology evolving faster than
we can name it.
